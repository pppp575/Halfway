What stays on your device

Your results, stats, badges, settings and callsign are saved in your browser’s local storage so the game remembers you. They never leave your device unless you use the leaderboard. Clearing your browser’s site data removes them.

The leaderboard

If the leaderboard is switched on, the game creates an anonymous account for your browser. We don’t ask for your name or email. For each round you play we store the pin you placed, your score, whether you played in hard mode and the time. We also store your generated callsign and any leagues you create or join. This data is hosted by Supabase, [region].

Other players see your callsign, scores and, after they finish a round, an anonymous dot for your pin. To remove your leaderboard data, open Settings and choose “Delete my leaderboard data”.
